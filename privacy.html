Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Capy Lab Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool operated by a single creator to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account. It is not a public service.

Information the app accesses

When the creator signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the basic profile information that TikTok provides for the authorized scopes (such as the display name). The app uses this only to upload videos to the creator's own account.

How information is used and stored

  • The access token is stored only on the creator's own computer and is used only to call the TikTok API.
  • The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about any other person.
  • The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Third parties

Information is exchanged only with TikTok, as needed to sign in and upload videos. Use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. The creator can also delete the stored token from their computer.
