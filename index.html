Capy Lab Uploader

Capy Lab Uploader is a small personal tool used by one creator to publish their own hand-drawn animated shorts (starring a capybara character named Capy) to their own TikTok account.

The tool connects to the creator's TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and uploads finished videos with the Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public, does not collect data from other users, and does not read or store anything about viewers.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok and authorizes the app.
  2. The creator chooses a finished video, a caption and the privacy level.
  3. The app uploads the video to the creator's own account.
